NEO CARWORKS INSURANCE Terms and Conditions

NEO CARWORKS provides a quotation and inquiry interface. A quotation shown by this website is an indicative calculation and is not itself an insurance policy or a confirmation of coverage.

Actual premiums, coverage, underwriting approval, deductibles, exclusions and policy conditions are subject to the applicable insurer's final assessment and policy wording.

By submitting an inquiry, you authorize NEO CARWORKS to use the information you provide to process the inquiry and communicate with you regarding the requested insurance quotation.

Please review the final insurer quotation and policy documents before purchasing coverage.

NEO CARWORKS INSURANCE Privacy Policy

NEO CARWORKS may collect the information you enter, including your name, contact details, vehicle information, selected coverage and quotation inquiry details.

The information is used to calculate an indicative quote, process your inquiry, contact you about your request and support advisor review.

We aim to keep submitted information reasonably protected and only use it for legitimate business and inquiry-related purposes. Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers or other highly sensitive information through the quotation form.

Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee insurance approval or policy issuance.
